## Analysis of doubly stochastic processes with long memory

This research action studied processes whose properties vary randomly or evolve in time,
including models with random diffusivity and Hurst exponent. The work concerned their
ergodic and statistical properties, estimation, measurement noise, and applications to
single-particle-tracking data.
