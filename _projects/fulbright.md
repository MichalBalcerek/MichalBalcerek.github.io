## Fulbright Senior Award

I have been awarded the Fulbright Senior Award 2026 for a research stay at Colorado State
University with Prof. Diego Krapf's group in the 2026/27 academic year.

The programme is now called the **Fulbright Polish Scholar Award**, starting with
the 2027 edition. My award was granted under the **Fulbright Senior Award** name.

The research concerns stochastic models of anomalous diffusion and statistical methods
for analysing experimental single-particle trajectories. Collaboration with an
interdisciplinary group connects mathematical modelling with biological experiments.
