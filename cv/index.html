Professional Summary

Assistant Professor at the Faculty of Pure and Applied Mathematics, Wrocław University of Science and Technology. My research concerns stochastic processes, anomalous diffusion, long memory, statistical inference, and the analysis of single-particle trajectories.

Teaching and Supervision

  • Teaching since 2013. Courses include computer simulation of stochastic processes, numerical methods, financial engineering, stochastic modelling of financial markets, probability and statistics software, differential equations, calculus, linear algebra, risk modelling, and insurance mathematics.
  • Thesis supervision. More than 50 BSc/BEng and MSc thesis projects supervised since 2018 in applied mathematics, stochastic processes, statistical inference, computational methods, and data analysis.
  • Doctoral supervision. Assistant supervisor to two doctoral candidates, including one in the Industrial Doctorate programme.
  • Teaching tools: Julia, Python, MATLAB, Mathematica, R, and SAS.
